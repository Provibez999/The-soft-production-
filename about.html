The Studio

SOFT International Studios is a Nigerian creative production company dedicated to cinematic visual storytelling and exceptional video production. Built around the power of visual expression, the studio transforms ideas into compelling visual experiences through creative direction, professional production and intentional cinematography.

The Founder

At the heart of its creative vision is Babalola “Soft” Oluwakeremomi, a Nigerian cinematographer, Director of Photography and visual storyteller based in Abuja. Since beginning his journey in cinematography in 2021, he has built experience across music videos, fashion, commercials, documentaries, events and branded productions.

Driven by a keen eye for composition, lighting, movement and storytelling, Soft believes that great visuals should do more than capture attention; they should communicate, evoke emotion and leave a lasting impression.

From commercials and music videos to documentaries, fashion films and creative campaigns, SOFT International Studios brings creative ideas to life with a commitment to visual excellence and purposeful storytelling.

We don’t just make films. We create visual experiences that stay with you.
